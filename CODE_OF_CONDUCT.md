# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
by reference. The canonical text is available at
<https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/>; it is adapted from the Contributor Covenant
(Copyright Organization for Ethical Source), licensed under
[CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). All participants, in issues, pull requests, discussions,
and any other project space, are expected to follow it.

In short: be respectful, give and accept constructive feedback gracefully, focus on what is best for the project, and
do not harass, demean, or exclude others.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour privately through the
[repository security advisory form](https://github.com/jonathan-vella/azure-gh-runners/security/advisories/new)
(choose a title starting with "Conduct:"), or by contacting the maintainer [@jonathan-vella](https://github.com/jonathan-vella)
through the contact options on their GitHub profile. Reports are handled in confidence by the maintainer, who may
respond with a warning, temporary or permanent removal from project spaces, or removal of content, following the
enforcement guidelines in the Contributor Covenant.
